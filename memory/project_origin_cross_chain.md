Opre (the proposed rename of CAM, 2026-11-01) and Brevity came out of brainstorming and hobby projects around blockchain cross-chain transactions, as the user said on 2026-11-01. Cross-actor transactions are planned as an Opre protocol feature, not a Brevity language feature.

**Why:** the design's emphasis on trust, signatures and atomic groups of writes traces back to this problem.

**How to apply:** in transaction design, don't assume a trusted coordinator across trust boundaries. In positioning, treat MCP's lack of atomicity across tools as a central contrast.
